# Mid-360 Formal Batch-1 preregistered future analysis

No formal ICP is authorized by this document alone. A future runner must authenticate `formal_batch1_lock.json`, its companion fingerprint, the unchanged preregistration, registries, bag/target/snapshot/geometry manifests, and backend parameter contract before any backend invocation.

Targets are built by direct merging of stationary MAP frames, finite XYZ and the frozen 0.1–200 m range filter, then 0.05 m deterministic voxelization. Registration, odometry, scan matching, and ICP are forbidden during target construction. Query snapshots reuse `phase_a_harness.mid360_pilot.split.select_query_frames` with quantiles 0.05 through 0.95, nearest unused sequence rank, earlier-rank tie breaking, and exactly ten snapshots per station. All 180 selections and their SHA bindings must freeze before ICP.

Geometry admission is registration blind at identity. A scene is RICH only if its medians simultaneously satisfy normalized lambda-min ≥ 0.18, condition number ≤ 3.0, and spectral entropy ≥ 0.90. A scene is WEAK only if medians simultaneously satisfy normalized lambda-min ≤ 0.12, condition number ≥ 6.0, and entropy ≤ 0.80. Otherwise it is INTERMEDIATE and not admitted. Scene medians are computed over all 30 frozen station snapshots; station-level rows remain available. Semantic candidate labels are not final classes.

The future backends are Open3D `0.19.0+b012259` and PCL `1.15.1`, bound to contract SHA256 `6a1ebdee6b34390f1430eab371e1c4108db1f124b59b7239d74785efa6474af9`. Translation-only coarse magnitudes are 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40, 0.80, 1.20, and 1.60 m; extension magnitudes are 2.40, 3.20, 4.80, and 6.40 m, with 6.4 m as the absolute cap. Every magnitude preserves weak/strong directions and positive/negative signs.

Recovery requires both final translation error ≤ 0.005 m and final rotation error ≤ 0.2 degrees. A success/failure bracket is refined by binary bisection until width ≤ 0.025 m or eight iterations. `NON_MONOTONIC_RECOVERY_PATTERN`, `CAPTURE_RADIUS_AMBIGUOUS`, `RIGHT_CENSORED`, and `LEFT_CENSORED` are retained without manual repair. Every run records initial correspondence count/fraction and `LOW_INITIAL_OVERLAP`. Sign-specific raw results, plus/minus capture radii, and asymmetry ratio remain permanent.

Primary endpoints are weak-direction and strong-direction capture radii. Primary comparisons are Weak versus Rich scenes under weak-direction perturbation, and weak versus strong direction within scene. Sign asymmetry, backend agreement, initial-overlap behavior, and non-monotonic profiles are secondary. H1 expects Weak-class scenes to have smaller capture radii than Rich-class scenes; H2 expects geometry-predicted weak directions to have smaller radii than strong directions. These are expectations, not forced outcomes.

Analysis respects `scene → station → snapshot → perturbation → backend`. Scene is the primary summary unit; station and snapshot quantify within-scene robustness/variance. FMB1 (3 Rich + 3 Weak) is a variance-estimation/feasibility set. Its 180 snapshots must never be reported as 180 independent samples; a possible Batch-2 is decided only from scene-level variance, between-scene effects, within-scene station variance, and backend reproducibility.
